# The Sundown Basin — location master list

A fictional 50s atomic-age wasteland of 48 locations. The map, its data and its generator are in the repo:
- `tools/worldmap/locations.py`: every location, route and landform; edit here.
- `tools/worldmap/gen_worldmap.py`: renders the map.
- `assets/worldmap/worldmap.png` and `locations.json`: what the game will load.

**Geography:**
- The Iron Spine Mountains wall off the west; Gallows Pass is the only road through.
- Pinewood Forest covers the north.
- The Kettle River runs from the north-west mountains across the farm plains to New Halcyon, then into Lake Mercy behind Hollister Dam.
- The Ochre Desert, the Bright Flats salt pan and the bomb crater (the Glow) fill the south.
- The Sunrise Hills rise in the east.

**Routes:**
- Interstate 9 runs east–west through Haven Flats.
- Highway 3 runs north–south.
- A railway crosses from New Halcyon through the Ironback Tunnel.

Danger levels: 1 safe … 5 deadly. Each location's map size is in tiles, and every location gets its own map.
